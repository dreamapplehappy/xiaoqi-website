Questions, answered.

Does TakeFive need the internet?

No. Everything runs locally on your Mac. Nothing is tracked, and nothing leaves your machine.

Is it free?

Yes — the core break reminder is free forever. Pro adds smart auto-pause and focus protection, unlocked with a one-time purchase.

Which Mac do I need?

TakeFive works on macOS 13 Ventura or later, on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Does it collect my data?

No. TakeFive doesn’t collect, store, or send any personal data. What happens on your Mac stays on your Mac.
